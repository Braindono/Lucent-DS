# Primary Direction

This is Lucent's locked, shipping identity — a merge of Concept A (Instrument) and Concept B (Atrium), replacing the earlier three-way exploration as the direction this system builds forward from. Concept A, B and C stay in the guide as the historical record of that exploration; nothing about them changed.

**What carried over from each.** From Instrument: the bare neumorphic base, corner brackets, the instrument-tick rule, mono readouts, and the sharp transport-button row (`radius-xs`). From Atrium: one floating glass panel over that base rather than a bare surface throughout, and a softer outer radius (`radius-sm`, a half-step down from Instrument's sharpest setting, nowhere near Atrium's `radius-lg`). The glow behind the glass panel is deliberately a single fixed instance, not a parallax field — Instrument's restraint checks Atrium's opulence rather than the two just sitting side by side.

**The accent hierarchy is now the point, not incidental.** Where Instrument spent indigo alone and Atrium spent cyan alone, this component spends all three locked accents with real roles: `accent-primary` (indigo) carries the fader fill, the record dot and the primary action; `accent-secondary` (violet) marks the live delta and outlines the secondary action; `accent-tertiary` (cyan) appears once, as the small "SYNCED" tag's dot — a status tell, never a fill, the same restraint this system already applied to `accent-b` in Concept C and to `accent-green` (now `accent-tertiary`) in the panel case study.

**Where the three locked accents come from.** `accent-primary` is Instrument's own indigo (`indigo-500` / `indigo-300`). `accent-secondary` is Aurora Console's violet (`violet-700` / `violet-300`) — Concept C itself doesn't carry over structurally, only this hue does. `accent-tertiary` is the cyan already living inside Concept C's own design, on its "SYNC" pill and second meter fill (`cyan-500` / `cyan-300`) — visually closer to teal than a pure hue-wheel green, which is why it reads as "the green in Aurora Console" even though the system never had a fourth accent hue. This same triad is now the panel case study's corrected tertiary too (see that component's own README).

Two theme-toggle conventions merge as well: Atrium's glass-track toggle shape, carrying Instrument's small indigo tell inside the knob rather than Atrium's cyan one — a small, deliberate signal that indigo, not cyan, is primary going forward.
